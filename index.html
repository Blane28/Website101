Established in 1997

Over 25 Years of Community Tradition

Founded in 1997, Ahwatukee Barbers was built on a simple philosophy: bring the timeless craft and meticulous attention to detail of a traditional New York Style Barber Shop right to the heart of Ahwatukee Foothills.

Nestled at the Elliot & Warner loop inside the Equestrian Center, our shop has grown to become a cornerstone for generations of local residents. From day one, we believed that a haircut should be an experience, not a chore. That is why every haircut includes a complete service package: beard trimming, eyebrow grooming, warm shaving cream neck razor cleanup, and a soothing hot towel treatment to finish.
